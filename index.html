Privacy Policy — Harish Penthouse Ads

Last updated: 20 October 2026

"Harish Penthouse Ads" is a private application operated by Valery Computers And Communication Ltd (Israel). It is used only to manage advertising and posts for the sale of one apartment (penthouse in Harish, Israel) on the Facebook Page "חריש פנטהאוז" and its linked Instagram and Threads accounts.

What data we process

  • Ad performance statistics from Meta (impressions, spend, clicks, conversations), aggregated and not linked to individuals.
  • Messages and comments that people send to the Page, only to reply to them and to schedule apartment viewings: name as shown by Meta, message text, and contact details the person chooses to share.

How we use it

Only to answer questions about the apartment, arrange viewings and measure the advertising campaign. We do not sell or share personal data with third parties and do not use it for any other purpose.

Retention and deletion

Conversation data is kept until the apartment is sold and then deleted, no later than 12 months. To request deletion of your data at any time, send a message to the Page "חריש פנטהאוז" on Facebook or email admin@valery.co.il, and it will be deleted within 30 days.

Contact
